# Analyst workplan and quality checklist

## Workplan
1. Define market scope and time period; distinguish domestic market from exports and broad industry turnover.
2. Collect FY25 audited company financials and investor presentations.
3. Normalise financials to ₹ crore and document any definition differences.
4. Calculate revenue growth and EBITDA margins; rank peers and inspect outliers.
5. Segment therapy areas and identify growth / share patterns.
6. Build a hypothesis tree for market growth, differentiated products, supply chain and operations.
7. Translate findings into an answer-first executive storyline.
8. Validate every figure, formula, unit, date and hyperlink before publishing.

## Quality checks
- [ ] FY24/FY25 periods aligned (year ended March 31, 2025)
- [ ] Consolidated vs standalone basis labelled
- [ ] ₹ crore vs ₹ billion vs USD billion not mixed
- [ ] EBITDA definition and margin denominator disclosed
- [ ] Market scope definitions disclosed
- [ ] All claims have a source URL
- [ ] Formula outputs spot-checked independently
- [ ] Recommendations labelled as hypotheses, not facts
- [ ] Presentation headlines state the insight, not only the topic
