# Indian Pharmaceutical Industry — Market Intelligence & Competitive Benchmarking

A consulting-style public-source research project designed to demonstrate entry-level analyst capabilities across industry research, market sizing, competitive intelligence, financial benchmarking, Excel modelling and executive communication.

## Deliverables
- `India_Pharma_Competitive_Benchmark.xlsx` — workbook with market snapshot, peer financials, therapy mix, strategic analysis, analyst toolkit and source register.
- `India_Pharma_Strategy_Executive_Deck.pptx` — 7-slide executive presentation.
- `data/` — structured CSV extracts used in the analysis.
- `references.md` — bibliography and scope notes.
- `analyst_workplan.md` — project workplan and analytical approach.

## Research scope
- Market context: FY26 market indicators and 2030 projections where publicly available.
- Company benchmark: FY25 consolidated financials for Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy's, Cipla, Lupin and Zydus Lifesciences.
- Therapeutic mix: selected therapy-area shares and historical growth indicators.
- Strategy: opportunities, risks, commercial and operational workstreams, and testable recommendations.

## Key analytical caveats
1. Broad domestic pharma market estimates and narrower domestic formulation/IPM sales estimates are different measures. They are shown separately and must not be added together.
2. Peer EBITDA definitions can vary by company. Margins are calculated as reported EBITDA divided by reported revenue and are directional, not perfectly accounting-comparable.
3. The peer group is selected for strategic relevance and scale; it is not a claim that these are the five largest Indian pharma companies under every ranking.
4. Therapy share and CAGR estimates are selected public-market indicators and may use different snapshots or scopes.
5. All recommendations are analyst hypotheses requiring validation; this is not investment advice.

## Methods demonstrated
- Market sizing concepts, CAGR and scenario framing
- Peer benchmarking, common-size and margin analysis
- Therapy segmentation and competitive positioning
- Hypothesis-driven analysis, MECE issue trees and answer-first communication
- Source triangulation, data-quality checks and assumption documentation
- Excel formulas, conditional formatting, charts and linked source register
- PowerPoint storyline and executive-ready synthesis
